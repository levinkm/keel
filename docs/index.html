The app
Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for people who want serious financial visibility without the overhead of enterprise accounting software.

Double-entry Ledger
Every transaction balances. Net worth, loan balances and cash flow fall out of the data automatically.
Investments
Stocks, ETFs, unit trusts, fixed deposits, treasury bills, SACCOs and pension schemes. Lot tracking with unrealised and realised gains.
Loan Engine
Real amortisation schedules, payment recording and interest calculations for both borrowed and lent loans.
Envelope Budgeting
Monthly plans with strategy allocation — 50/30/20 or custom ratios. Rollover support and actual vs planned comparison.
Physical Assets
Vehicles, buildings, land and machinery. Depreciation schedules and insurance expiry tracking.
Net Worth Projections
Forward trajectory modelling with income growth, expense growth and savings targets. Loan payoffs factored in automatically.
PDF Reports
Export P&L statements, budget summaries, loan schedules, investment portfolios and asset registers.
Cloud Sync
Optional sync with Google sign-in. Offline-first — everything works locally. Sync is additive, never a replacement.
Keyboard First
Native macOS menu bar, transparent title bar, live shortcut recording. Every action reachable from the keyboard.
Design Principles

Built on four ideas.

01
Your data belongs to you
Everything is stored locally in SQLite. Nothing leaves your machine unless you choose to sync it.
02
Desktop is the right surface
Deep data entry, multi-panel layouts, keyboard shortcuts and PDF reports are all first-class citizens.
03
Correctness over convenience
Money is stored as integer minor units throughout. No floating point arithmetic, no rounding errors.
04
Clean, minimal UI
No card borders. No tinted selection backgrounds. Visual hierarchy through typography and spacing.
