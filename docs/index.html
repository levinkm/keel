Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for people who want serious financial visibility without the overhead of enterprise accounting software.

📒
Double-entry Ledger
Every transaction balances. Net worth, loan balances and cash flow fall out of the data automatically. Asset, liability, income and expense account types.
📈
Investments
Holdings across stocks, ETFs, unit trusts, fixed deposits, treasury bills, SACCOs and pension schemes. Lot tracking with unrealised and realised gain calculations.
🏦
Loan Engine
Borrowed and lent loan tracking. Real amortisation schedules, payment event recording, interest calculations and settlement quotes on demand.
📊
Envelope Budgeting
Monthly period plans with strategy allocation — 50/30/20, Commitments First, Kakeibo or custom ratios. Rollover support and actual vs planned comparison.
🏠
Physical Assets
Track vehicles, buildings, land and machinery. Depreciation schedules, insurance expiry tracking and project linkage for cost attribution.
🔮
Net Worth Projections
Forward trajectory modelling with configurable income growth, expense growth and savings targets. Loan payoff schedules factored in automatically.
📄
PDF Reports
Export profit and loss statements, budget summaries, loan schedules, investment portfolios and asset registers. Full Unicode font support.
☁️
Cloud Sync
Optional Supabase-powered sync with Google sign-in. Offline-first — everything works locally. Sync is additive, never a replacement for local data.
⌨️
Keyboard First
Native macOS menu bar, transparent title bar, live shortcut recording. Every action reachable from the keyboard. No bottom sheets — dialogs only.
Design Principles

Built on three ideas.

Every decision in Keel traces back to one of these.

01
Your data belongs to you
Everything is stored locally in SQLite. Nothing leaves your machine unless you choose to sync it. No accounts required to use the app.
02
Desktop is the right surface
Deep data entry, multi-panel layouts, keyboard shortcuts and PDF reports are all first-class citizens. Mobile is a companion, not the primary surface.
03
Correctness over convenience
Money is stored as integer minor units throughout the entire stack. No floating point arithmetic, no rounding errors. Double-entry ensures every transaction balances.
04
Clean, minimal UI
No card borders. No tinted selection backgrounds. No blur effects on content. Visual hierarchy through typography and spacing, not decoration.
